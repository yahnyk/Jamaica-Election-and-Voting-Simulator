Polling Day

Try Casting a Ballot

Polls in Jamaica are open 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Here's what happens when a voter walks in. Click through each step.

1. Verify your identity

A presiding officer checks your ID and looks for indelible ink already on your finger, so nobody can vote twice.

2. Receive your ballot

Once you're verified, you're handed an official ballot paper listing every candidate in your constituency.

3. Mark it in private

In a private booth, mark an X next to the one candidate you're voting for. Try it below.

4. Deposit the ballot

Fold your ballot and place it in the sealed ballot box in full view of the polling staff.

📥

5. Dip your finger in ink

Last step: dip a finger in indelible ink. It won't wash off for days — proof you've already voted.

🎓 What you just practiced

Each seat went to whoever got the most votes in that constituency — that's First-Past-the-Post. A party doesn't need 50% to win a seat, just more votes than any single rival. The party with the most seats overall — not the most total votes nationwide — forms the government, and its leader becomes Prime Minister.
